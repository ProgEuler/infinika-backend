How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Study Pulse account directly from the app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Study Pulse app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon or your profile).
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your decision when prompted.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

⚠ Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Assessment history and scores
  • Course enrollments and progress
  • Booking history and scheduled sessions
  • Push notification preferences

Your data will be deleted within 30 days of your request. During this period, your account will be deactivated and inaccessible.

Need Help?

If you're unable to delete your account through the app, or if you have any questions, please contact us through the app's Help & Support section.
